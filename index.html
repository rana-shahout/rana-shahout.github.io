Rana Shahout

Postdoctoral Fellow · MIT CSAIL

I build systems that make AI faster, more efficient, and easier to run. My research brings together LLM serving, multi-agent infrastructure, and algorithms that learn from the models they support.

I am a postdoctoral fellow at MIT CSAIL. Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Technion, where I also completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. Before academia, I worked as a senior software engineer at Mellanox, now part of NVIDIA.
